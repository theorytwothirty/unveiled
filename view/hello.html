UNVEILED

A little more about us.

Connection begins with curiosity.

Let’s explore what we desire, what makes us feel safe, and what we’d love others to know about us.

Take a few minutes to share as much or as little as you like. You decide which answers the group sees.

Curiosity is welcome. Honesty is sexy. Consent is everything.

Your answers aren’t promises, invitations, or consent. You can skip any question.

Privacy

Everything you share is encrypted on your phone with the group password before it leaves, and only people with the same password can read it. Answers you keep private never leave your device at all. Sexual health and After Dark answers are private unless you choose otherwise.

Please don’t screenshot or pass on anyone’s answers, and never share the password outside the group. Nothing here is used to train AI.
